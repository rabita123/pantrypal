## Promotional text (170 chars)
Snap a receipt and PantryPal shows what to use first and what to cook tonight — so less food, and less money, goes in the bin.

## Description opening (first 3 lines are all most people read)
Stop throwing food away.
Snap a grocery receipt and PantryPal fills your pantry, shows what to use first, and suggests what to cook tonight with it.
No typing. No 30-minute setup. Useful in about a minute.

## What's New (next version)
- Brand-new "Use first" home screen: see exactly what to eat before it goes off
- Scan a receipt or tap what you have — set up in seconds
- Recipes ranked by what expires soonest, with one-tap "add what's missing"
- Shopping list warns you before you buy something you already own
- One evening reminder instead of a notification per item
- Barcode scanning and quick add are now always free

## Things to avoid (review + trust)
- Do not claim user counts, star ratings, or "% less waste" numbers that aren't measured.
- Savings figures shown in-app are estimates (marked "~") unless the user entered prices.
